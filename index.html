    Reminder

    A notification appears at that time if MoodMap is open in a tab and you haven't checked in yet. Browsers don't allow reminders from a closed web page without a server, and MoodMap has none.

    Your data

    Everything is stored in this browser's localStorage. Nothing is uploaded, and there are no accounts, analytics or trackers.

    Demo entries are marked as demo and never replace your real check-ins. “Remove demo data” deletes only them.

    Passcode lock (optional)

    Asks for a passcode when MoodMap opens. It's a privacy screen for a shared device, not encryption; anyone with access to the browser's storage could still read the data.

    Custom tags

    Removing a custom tag here hides it from the picker; past entries keep it.

    About

    MoodMap is a free, open-source mood and energy journal. Check in once a day, see your year as colored pixels, and spot patterns in your own entries.

    MoodMap is not medical advice and can't diagnose anything. Insights only show what tends to happen together in your entries. If you're struggling, please talk to someone you trust or a professional. In the US you can call or text 988 or visit 988lifeline.org (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, 24/7). Outside the US, contact your local emergency or crisis service.
